Install, and the first launch

Install

Drag openMemo onto the Applications folder in this window. That is the whole install. Getting past the first launch is one line in Terminal, or four clicks.

Then macOS blocks it once, and that is expected

Apple has never checked openMemo. macOS refuses the first launch of every app it cannot vouch for, and makes you allow it by hand. Nothing is wrong with the download, and this is not something openMemo can turn off from its side.

The one line way

A browser marks what it downloads with a hidden quarantine tag, and that tag is what macOS reacts to. Remove it and openMemo simply opens. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal, press return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenMemo.app

Terminal lives in Applications/Utilities, or press Command-Space and type its name. The line prints nothing when it works, which is the normal outcome. Run it again after each update.

The click-only way

Four steps, once per version

For anyone who would rather not touch Terminal. Every one of these dialogs is macOS talking about an app it cannot check, and none of them mean the download is damaged.

  1. Open openMemo from Applications

    macOS refuses, and this dialog offers no way through. Click Done. Do not click Move to Bin.

    “OpenMemo” Not Opened
    Apple could not verify “OpenMemo” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
    DoneMove to Bin

  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security

    Apple menu, System Settings, Privacy & Security in the sidebar. Scroll down to Security. openMemo is named there, with a button beside it. Click Open Anyway.

    “OpenMemo” was blocked to protect your Mac.
    Open Anyway
    That line only appears after step 1, and it fades after a while. If it is not there, try opening the app again.

  3. Confirm with Touch ID or your password

    macOS checks that you own the Mac.

    You are attempting to open an app that may cause harm to your Mac
    Touch ID or enter an administrator’s name and password to continue with Privacy & Security.
    Use Password…Cancel

  4. Say yes one last time

    A final dialog appears, this one with a way through. Click Open Anyway. openMemo starts, and it starts normally from now on.

    Open “OpenMemo”?
    Apple is not able to verify that it is free from malware that could harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
    Move to BinOpen AnywayDone

After the first launch

If you set a PIN lock

openMemo keeps the PIN as a hash in its own settings file, inside your library folder. No keychain, no login password, no access to anything else on your Mac.

Updating from 3.22.0 or older? Those builds did keep the PIN in the keychain, which is why macOS asked for your login password after every update. Reading that old PIN back needs the same panel, so openMemo throws it away instead and says so on first launch. Set a new PIN in Settings, under App lock. Nothing else is touched.

Updating later

openMemo checks GitHub for new versions and offers to download the new .dmg for you. When one arrives:

  1. Quit openMemo first

    Command-Q. The backend holds the database open.

  2. Drag the new openMemo onto Applications and replace

    Finder asks whether to replace. Say yes.

  3. Allow it again

    A fresh download carries a fresh block, so page one applies to every update, not only the first install. The one line is the quickest way through:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenMemo.app

Your library, and the project

Your library is not inside the app

Everything you keep lives in ~/Library/Application Support/OpenMemo/, so replacing the app replaces the program and nothing else. openMemo also copies the library into its backups folder before the first launch of a new version.

Never delete that folder to reinstall cleanly. That folder is your library. Throwing away the app is harmless. Throwing away that folder is not.

About openMemo

One person builds this. It is free and open source, it has no account, no subscription and no telemetry, and everything you save stays on your Mac. It gets better because people use it and say what is missing.
